You obtain tender documents yourself, directly from the issuing company, at your own cost and effort. Tender Gateway never supplies them.

Using Tender Gateway gives no automatic right to take part in a tender. Eligibility is decided solely by the issuing company.

Tender Gateway has no role in tender evaluation or award. Consultancy does not guarantee shortlisting or contract award.

6. Limitation of liability

Tender Gateway is not liable for consequential damages, loss of profit, or lost business opportunity arising from use of the platform or its consultancy services. Where liability cannot be excluded, it is capped at the fees paid in the preceding 12 months.

7. Paid consultancy scope

Consultancy covers proposal preparation quality, clarity and compliance only. No fee is payable and no work begins until you separately accept a specific quote.

8. Governing law

Disputes concerning a specific tender are governed by the law of the jurisdiction where the issuing organisation resides.

9. Legitimate purpose and anti-bribery

A request for consultancy is a genuine and lawful request for professional help to prepare a tender proposal, and nothing more. Our consultancy is strictly limited to the quality, clarity and compliance of your own written submission.

Privacy Notice

Last updated 23 November 2026. Baca dalam Bahasa Malaysia

This notice explains how Pacific Edge Trading Sdn Bhd (Registration No. 202501031124 (162536-A)), the operator of Tender Gateway ("we", "us"), handles personal data under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

1. What we collect

  • When you request a consultancy quote: company name, country of registration, contact person's name, email address, the tender you selected, the level of support you need and any notes you give us.
  • On your device: the reference numbers of your requests are saved in your browser so you can find them again under "My requests". You can clear them by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider records basic connection data such as IP addresses to run and secure the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Browsing the tender list does not require you to give us any personal data.

2. Why we use it

  • To review your request, prepare and email a quote, and communicate with you about it.
  • To provide the consultancy service if you accept a quote, and to keep business and tax records.
  • To screen requests against our anti-bribery terms and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

3. Is it required?

Company name, country, contact person and email are required to send you a quote. If you do not provide them, we cannot process your request. Other details are optional.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run Tender Gateway, such as our website hosting and email providers, and with authorities where the law requires it. We never send your details to the organisations that issue the tenders. Some of our providers store data on servers outside Malaysia; we use providers who protect it with appropriate safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and then delete it.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal data, ask us to stop using it, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@pacificedgetrading.com. We will reply within 21 days.

7. Contact

Pacific Edge Trading Sdn Bhd · info@pacificedgetrading.com

2. Tujuan penggunaan

  • Untuk menyemak permintaan anda, menyediakan dan menghantar sebut harga melalui e-mel, serta berhubung dengan anda mengenainya.

  • Untuk menyaring permintaan mengikut terma anti-rasuah kami dan mematuhi kewajipan undang-undang.

Kami tidak menjual data peribadi anda atau menggunakannya untuk pengiklanan pihak ketiga.

Nama syarikat, negara, orang untuk dihubungi dan e-mel adalah wajib untuk menghantar sebut harga. Tanpanya, kami tidak dapat memproses permintaan anda. Butiran lain adalah pilihan.

Hanya penyedia perkhidmatan yang membantu kami mengendalikan Tender Gateway, seperti penyedia hos laman web dan e-mel, serta pihak berkuasa apabila dikehendaki oleh undang-undang. Kami tidak sekali-kali menghantar butiran anda kepada organisasi yang mengeluarkan tender. Sesetengah penyedia kami menyimpan data pada pelayan di luar Malaysia; kami menggunakan penyedia yang melindunginya dengan langkah keselamatan yang sewajarnya.

5. Tempoh simpanan

Kami menyimpan data peribadi hanya selama yang diperlukan untuk tujuan di atas atau seperti yang dikehendaki oleh undang-undang, dan kemudian memadamnya.

Anda boleh meminta untuk mengakses atau membetulkan data peribadi anda, meminta kami berhenti menggunakannya, atau menarik balik persetujuan anda, dengan menghantar e-mel ke info@pacificedgetrading.com. Kami akan membalas dalam tempoh 21 hari.
